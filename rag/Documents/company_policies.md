# AcmeCorp Employee Policies & Procedures

## Leave Policy

### Paid Time Off (PTO)

- All full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per calendar year
- PTO accrues at 1.67 days per month
- Maximum carryover: 5 unused days to the next year (expires March 31)
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for 3+ days
- Single-day PTO can be requested with 48 hours notice
- Submit PTO requests through https://hr.acmecorp.com/time-off

### Sick Leave

- 10 days of sick leave per year (separate from PTO)
- Sick leave does not carry over — use it or lose it
- For absences of 3+ consecutive days, a doctor's note is required
- Notify your manager before your shift starts (email + Slack message)

### Parental Leave

- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid leave
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption
- Notify HR at least 30 days before expected leave start date

### Work From Home Policy

- Hybrid model: minimum 3 days in office per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday)
- Full remote exceptions require VP-level approval
- International remote work requires Legal and Tax team approval (submit request 30 days in advance)
- Home office equipment stipend: $500 one-time setup allowance

## Expense Policy

### Expense Submission

- All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase
- Submit via https://expenses.acmecorp.com
- Receipts required for all expenses over $25
- Reimbursement processed within 10 business days

### Travel Expenses

- Domestic flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours
- International flights: business class allowed for flights over 8 hours
- Hotel: up to $250/night for domestic, $350/night for international
- Meals: up to $75/day for domestic travel, $100/day for international
- Rental car: compact or midsize only, unless traveling with 3+ colleagues

### Office Expenses

- Software subscriptions under $50/month: manager approval only
- Software subscriptions $50-500/month: director approval required
- Hardware purchases: go through IT procurement (see Hardware Request process)
- Conference attendance: requires manager approval and budget allocation

### Client Entertainment

- Meals with clients: up to $150 per person
- Events/tickets: requires VP approval regardless of amount
- Always include attendee names and business purpose in expense report

## Onboarding Checklist for New Employees

### Week 1

- [ ] Complete HR paperwork in BambooHR
- [ ] Set up your AcmeCorp email account
- [ ] Install required software via Software Center
- [ ] Complete mandatory security training (2 hours)
- [ ] Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- [ ] Join your team's Slack channels
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 meetings with your direct team members
- [ ] Read the Employee Handbook at https://handbook.acmecorp.com

### Week 2

- [ ] Complete compliance training modules (4 hours total)
- [ ] Get building access badge from Security desk (Room 101)
- [ ] Set up VPN access for remote work
- [ ] Complete your profile on the internal directory
- [ ] Attend new hire orientation (every Monday at 10 AM)

### First Month

- [ ] Complete all required training modules
- [ ] Schedule a 30-day check-in with your manager
- [ ] Submit your home office equipment request (if hybrid/remote)
- [ ] Join at least one Employee Resource Group (ERG)
- [ ] Review your team's documentation and runbooks

## IT Security Policies

### Acceptable Use Policy

- Company devices are for business use; limited personal use is permitted
- Never install unauthorized software — use the Software Center
- Never share your credentials with anyone, including IT staff
- Report suspicious emails to security@acmecorp.com
- Lock your computer when stepping away (Windows + L)

### Data Classification

- **Public**: Marketing materials, blog posts, job descriptions
- **Internal**: Team documents, project plans, meeting notes
- **Confidential**: Customer data, financial reports, HR records
- **Restricted**: Encryption keys, access credentials, legal documents

### Incident Reporting

- Security incidents: email security@acmecorp.com AND call ext. 9911
- Data breach suspected: immediately notify your manager and Security team
- Lost/stolen device: report within 1 hour to IT Helpdesk (ext. 5555)
- Phishing attempt: forward the email to phishing@acmecorp.com (don't click any links)

### Remote Work Security

- Always use the VPN when accessing company resources remotely
- Never use public Wi-Fi without VPN
- Enable full-disk encryption on your laptop (should be enabled by default)
- Don't access company data on personal devices unless enrolled in MDM
